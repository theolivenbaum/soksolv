# Benchmark hardware (2026-11-14/15)

Six identical DigitalOcean dedicated-CPU droplets ran both solvers. Each benchmark job got 8 of the 16 vCPUs
(pinned with taskset), a 15 GB memory cap (systemd scope MemoryMax=15G, MemorySwapMax=0) and 600 s
(killed at 600.5 s). Two jobs ran per droplet at a time, one on each half of the CPUs. Captured from one
droplet (all six were identical: same CPU model, core count, memory, OS and kernel).
